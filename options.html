Read Later — Options

Choose which quick-save entry points show up while you browse.

Entry points

On-page floating save button
Show a small save button in the bottom-right corner of every page you visit.
Right-click "Add to Reading List" menu
Show "Add link/page to Reading List" when you right-click a page or a link.
Other ways to save still work regardless of these settings — the toolbar icon, the keyboard shortcut, and right-clicking the toolbar icon itself for the Reading List Manager.
