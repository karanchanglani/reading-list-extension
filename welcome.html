Welcome to Read Later

Four ways to save a page, all pointing at the same list.

1 The toolbar icon

Click the book-plus icon next to your address bar to save the current page and see your full reading list — search, mark items read, and delete, all from that one popup. If you don't see it, click the puzzle-piece "Extensions" icon and pin Read Later so it stays visible.

2 Keyboard shortcuts

Two shortcuts, the same combo on every platform:

ShortcutDoes what
Ctrl+Shift+S / MacCtrl+Shift+SInstantly saves the current page, with an on-page toast confirming it.
Ctrl+Shift+L / MacCtrl+Shift+LOpens your reading list — same as clicking the toolbar icon.

Either can be reassigned any time from Options → Manage shortcuts.

3 The floating save button

A small circular button appears in the bottom-right corner of every page, and always reflects that page's real saved/read state:

GestureResult
ClickSaves the page (indigo → orange).
Double-click ↔ Toggles read/unread, either direction.
Hold for 2.5sRemoves the page from your list — a ring sweeps around the button as you hold; release early and nothing happens.

Don't want it? Turn it off any time in Options.

4 The Options page

Open it from the gear icon in the popup, or by right-clicking the toolbar icon → Options. From there you can turn the floating button or right-click menu off, manage the keyboard shortcut, and export or import your reading list as a JSON or Pocket CSV file.
